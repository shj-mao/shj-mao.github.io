## Biography

Shanjun Mao, born in September 1993 in Zhangjiajie, Hunan Province, received his Ph.D. in Statistics from The Chinese University of Hong Kong. He is currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Statistics and Data Science, College of Finance and Statistics, Hunan University, and also serves as Deputy Head of the department.
